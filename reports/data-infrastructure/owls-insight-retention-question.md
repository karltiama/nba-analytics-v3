I plan to use your historical NBA player-prop API for internal analytics/model research. May I retain the API responses I retrieve during an active subscription/trial in a private S3 archive for continued internal use after the subscription or trial ends? The data would not be resold or redistributed.

## What the published Terms of Service say (effective 2026-08-24)

This is not a legal conclusion. It is a reading of the public text at https://owlsinsight.com/terms.

**Redistribution / resale**

Section 7 (Acceptable Use) says you shall not “Share, resell, redistribute, or sublicense API data or API keys to any third party without our prior written consent.”

**Automated usage**

Section 7 also forbids scraping/crawling or using automated means to access the Service “outside of the provided API endpoints,” circumventing rate limits, and usage patterns that maximize throughput at the expense of other users even if technically within stated limits (Section 8).

**Commercial / research use**

Section 10 says the Service provides sports odds data “strictly for informational, analytical, and research purposes.” Owls Insight is not a sportsbook.

**Retention after trial or subscription ends**

The Terms do **not** explicitly grant a right to keep retrieved API responses after a trial or paid subscription ends. Section 6.3 says cancelling during a trial ends API access immediately. Section 16 says that upon termination, the right to access and use the Service ceases immediately and API keys are revoked. Section 9 grants a limited license to access and use the Service according to the Terms and plan; it does not separately spell out post-termination archive rights.

Section 9 also says Owls does not claim ownership of the underlying odds data published by third-party sportsbooks, and that use of such data is subject to the Terms and applicable laws.

**Ask them in writing before assuming the S3 tape may outlive the trial.**
